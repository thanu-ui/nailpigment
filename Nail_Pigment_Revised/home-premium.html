Every detail has a purpose. Explore the thoughtful steps behind a beautiful manicure, from nail shaping and cuticle care to your favorite finishing touch.

Begin with clean hands and gentle preparation for a fresh, comfortable manicure.

02

Shape and refine

Choose an almond, oval, square or squoval shape and refine the edges for a neat appearance.

03

04
