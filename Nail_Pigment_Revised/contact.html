QUICK ANSWERS

Appointment FAQs.

Is my appointment confirmed after I submit the form?

No. This demo displays a local confirmation message only. The studio must confirm your request through a connected service or direct communication.

Can I request a particular design?

Yes. Choose a design from the Gallery page or describe your inspiration in the special notes field.

Can I upload a reference photo?

You can select an image in this demo, but it is not uploaded. A secure file-upload service is required to send it to the studio.

Are the displayed time slots available?

No. They are preferred times only. Live availability requires a booking backend or appointment provider.

Where can I find prices?

Prices and service durations must be confirmed by the studio.
