THE NAIL PIGMENT METHOD

The art behind every nail design.

Every detail has a purpose. Explore the thoughtful steps behind creating a beautiful, balanced nail-art look.

01

Choose your nail-art goal

Decide your inspiration, color palette, nail shape and the style you want to achieve.

02

Prepare the canvas

Shape and prepare the nails, then apply the appropriate base for your chosen finish.

03

Build the design

Apply the base color and carefully layer the colors or patterns that bring your idea to life.

04

Add the finishing details

Refine fine lines, accents and decorative elements to give the design its character.

05

Seal and admire

Complete the design with the appropriate top coat for a polished final appearance.
